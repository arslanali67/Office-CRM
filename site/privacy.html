What this covers

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you contact us by email, Facebook Messenger or Instagram messages, or when we contact you about our services.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us and our replies (text and attachments you choose to send).

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions, handle bookings and orders, and give customer support.

  • To keep records we need for our business and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use your messages for advertising.

Automated assistance

We use an AI service (Anthropic) to help read messages and draft replies. Messages are sent to that service only to produce a reply for us. Replies to sensitive messages (complaints, refunds, legal matters) are always written or approved by a person.

How long we keep it

We keep conversations for as long as needed for customer support and our legal obligations, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner (see below).
