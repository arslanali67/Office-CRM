# Employee guide (one page)

**Log in**: open `https://crm.<company-domain>` on your computer or phone. Use your own email/username and the password you were given; change it on first login. Never share it.

**Your dashboard** shows only your things: *Check In / Out*, *My tasks*, *My leads*, *My conversations needing a reply*, *My messages needing a reply*, and your calendar.

**Check in / out** (every working day)
- Press **Check in** when you start, **Check out** when you finish. The server records the time and your network address (this is how the office network rule works; the company told you about this in advance).
- If it says you are already checked in or you are outside the office network, tell the owner; do not try again from another place.
- Forgot to check out? The system closes the shift at midnight and marks it for the owner to correct.

**Tasks**
- Open a task from *My tasks*, press **Start** or set the status to **Started**, and **Completed** when done. Add a comment in the stream if something is unclear. You get a notification when a task is assigned to you and a message each morning for overdue tasks.

**Emails and messages assigned to you**
- *Emails*: open the email. The **AI reply** box shows what the AI suggests. Edit the text, then press **Send AI draft** (or **Reply** to write your own). Never send a price that is not in the knowledge base.
- *Instagram / Facebook*: open the conversation. Read the chat, then write in the box and press **Send**, or **Use AI draft** first and edit it.
- Look at the line above the chat: you can answer a customer only **within 24 hours of their last message** (a Meta rule). After that Send is blocked until they write again.
- Complaints, refunds and anything legal always need a person. Be polite, say what you will do and by when.

**Leads**: *My leads* lists the leads assigned to you. When a lead replies "interested" you get a task: call or write back the same day.

**Phone**: everything works in the phone browser; add the page to your home screen.

**Problems?** Tell the owner what you pressed and what you saw (a screenshot helps). Do not use other tools for customer messages: the CRM must keep every message.
